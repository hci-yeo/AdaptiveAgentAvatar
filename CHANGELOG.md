# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (November 2026)

The first public test version.

- **Windows desktop apps:**
  - ChatGPT Codex, Doubao, Yuanbao, 千问 (Qianwen) and Cursor, with her inside the app.
  - Claude and Kimi, with her in a see-through window beside the chat box. Each chat keeps its own expert.
  - DeepSeek Harness, as a plugin.
- **Browser extension** for Chrome and Edge: ChatGPT, Claude, Doubao (and Dola), Kimi, Qwen, Qianwen and Yuanbao, plus almost any other AI chat site you add from its toolbar button.
- **Experimental:** an Android app and a macOS app.
- **An expert for each topic:** 16 topics, picked on your own device by a small local AI model (EmbeddingGemma). Follow-up questions keep the expert, and small talk brings back her everyday look.
- **Fully offline:** questions are never saved or sent anywhere. If the model can't load, she uses keyword lists.
- **Three styles:** different people, Mandy (小曼) changing outfits, or Whale Girl (大肥鱼). Every expert has her own name, on a small plate under her.
- **Ways to show her:** beside the chat box or in a corner, in each AI reply, or floating on the desktop (pet mode). She reacts while the AI thinks, writes and finishes.
- **Options:** four sizes, an English or Chinese interface, and a rainbow ring around the chat box.
- **Tray icon:** opens every installed app with her, restarts her or turns her off, and links to a feedback form.
- **Setup:** `Install.bat` adds the shortcuts (run it again after moving the folder), and `Uninstall.bat` removes everything it added.
